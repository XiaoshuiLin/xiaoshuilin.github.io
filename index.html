About me

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Department of Physics at Washington University in St. Louis, working with Prof. Chuanwei Zhang.

My current research focuses on designing schemes for entanglement-enhanced quantum sensing. My research spans localization, nonequilibrium quantum dynamics, and quantum technologies. I am interested in how disorder, interactions, and driving shape quantum systems, and in using atomic systems for quantum sensing and quantum networks.

I received my Ph.D. in Physics from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2024, advised by Prof. Ming Gong.

Research interests

  • Anderson localization, many-body localization, and mobility edges
  • Bose–Einstein condensates and nonequilibrium dynamics
  • Non-Hermitian physics and phase transitions in open systems
  • Quantum sensing and squeezing
  • Quantum networks with neutral atoms
