Interests & directions

Research

My research spans localization, nonequilibrium dynamics, quantum sensing, and quantum networks.

Localization and critical phases

I study Anderson localization, many-body localization, and mobility edges in disordered and quasiperiodic systems. My work considers coupled chains, network models, and random matrices, with a focus on critical phases and the interplay of extended and localized states.

Related work

Hidden Self Duality and Exact Mobility Edges in Quasiperiodic Network Models

Fate of Localization in a Coupled Free Chain and a Disordered Chain

General Approach to the Critical Phase with Coupled Quasiperiodic Chains

Bose–Einstein condensates and nonequilibrium dynamics

I am interested in the dynamics of ultracold atoms, including coupled condensates and periodically driven systems. My work includes effective Hamiltonians in driven Bose–Josephson junctions and soliton and traveling-wave solutions in coupled one-dimensional condensates.

Related work

Soliton and Traveling-Wave Solutions in Coupled One-Dimensional Condensates

Different Effective Hamiltonians in a Periodic-Driven Bose–Josephson Junction

Open systems and non-Hermitian physics

My interests include non-Hermitian physics and phase transitions in open quantum systems. Related work explores the simulation of open quantum systems on universal quantum computers and criticality in the cavity-Ising model.

Related work

Simulation of Open Quantum Systems on Universal Quantum Computers

Unilateral Criticality and Phase Transition in the Cavity-Ising Model

Quantum sensing and squeezing

I study quantum sensing and squeezing, including multiparameter squeezing with qudits. I also collaborate on quantum sensing with nitrogen-vacancy centers in diamond.

Related work

Single-Ensemble Multiparameter Squeezing with Qudits

A Deep-Learning-Boosted Framework for Quantum Sensing with Nitrogen-Vacancy Centers in Diamond

Quantum networks with neutral atoms

I am interested in quantum networks built from neutral atoms. My work explores cold atomic ensembles as quantum antennas for distributed networks of single-atom arrays.

Related work

Cold Atomic Ensembles as Quantum Antennas for Distributed Networks of Single-Atom Arrays
